## The Protagonist

An ordinary person living a mundane, repetitive life. Nothing special, no destiny, just pure boredom with the daily grind.

## The Crossing

One night, they take a wrong turn - a door that shouldn't exist, an elevator floor that isn't on the map, a path that wasn't there before. They step through and find themselves elsewhere.

## The Choice

A hooded figure waits. No explanations, just two pills:
- **Blue**: Return home. Forget everything. Resume your life.
- **Red**: Enter the fight. No guarantees.

Most choose blue. You chose red - not because you're a hero, but because you were bored.

## Hollow

A small fantasy town nestled in endless meadows. Home to craftspeople who keep the town running - blacksmiths, merchants, farmers. They live simple lives between crises.

## The Threat

**The Abyss** manifests as portals throughout the land. Dungeons form around them. If left unchecked, the creatures inside spill out into the meadows.

The people of Hollow learned to fight back. They established trials - dungeons and arenas - to train warriors and keep the darkness at bay.

But they were losing. They needed someone who could grow strong enough to end it permanently.

## The Truth

The Abyss isn't evil. It's a test.

It only consumes worlds that stagnate, that stop fighting, that give up. The portals, the trials, the bosses - all of it is judgment.

Defeat the Abyss's champion and you prove your world deserves to survive. The portals remain, but dormant. Silent. Watching, but no longer hungry.

## The End

The hero returns to a town that no longer lives in fear. The meadows stay peaceful. The people can finally just... live.

What the hero does next is their choice.
